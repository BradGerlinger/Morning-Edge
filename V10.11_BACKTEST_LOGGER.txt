15 MINUTE EDGE V10.11 — FULL-SIGNAL SETTLEMENT LOGGER

WHAT CHANGED
- BTC Value entry/qualification rules are unchanged.
- Push notifications are still only sent for qualified VALUE trades.
- Every recorded BTC 15-minute signal, including PASS, is now checked against the exact Kalshi ticker after settlement.
- PASS signals do NOT count as live/paper trades in dashboard win rate or P/L.
- New CSV columns:
    hypothetical_result
    hypothetical_pnl
- outcome, settlement_source, kalshi_result, and settled_ts are populated for PASS rows after official settlement.
- Existing historical PASS rows in the Render database are progressively backfilled through the paced settlement loop.
- /api/stats adds:
    all_signals_settled
    passes_settled
    backtest_settlement_backlog

WHY
This allows full price-window and signal-factor backtesting across rejected and accepted signals without contaminating the current strategy statistics.

DEPLOYMENT
Upload/replace the repository files with this package and redeploy on Render. Keep the existing persistent disk/database and existing Render environment variables.
